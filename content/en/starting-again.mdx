Hello everyone, it's been a while — this is Shotaro. I hope you've all been well.

I haven't posted a video in quite some time, so some of you may have been wondering where I disappeared to.

Starting today, I'm going to begin sharing my thoughts in writing, and I'll also be easing back into social media little by little.

## On what I've been up to

So what was I doing while I wasn't posting videos? Mostly, taking things in.

My style up until now has mostly been output-driven, whether through SNS, Japanese education, that kind of thing.

But somewhere in the back of my mind, I had this vague feeling that my inner drawers were running a little empty.

On top of that, we're living through a time of incredibly rapid change. Society, technology, the world itself — everything is shifting at a remarkable pace.

So I spent this time reading, watching, and thinking. Things like philosophy, geopolitics, behavioral economics, how society is changing, how we'll work and live in the future — all the books and videos I'd been curious about but never got around to.

AI in particular was something I hadn't really kept up with for the past year or two. I hadn't been following how far the cutting edge had actually come.

I felt I seriously needed to catch up, so I started trying out AI tools that caught my interest and using Claude Code to vibe-code all kinds of websites and small apps.

It turned out to be way more fun than I expected, and before I knew it, I was completely hooked.

And after all that time taking things in, I started wanting to put something out again. I found I had a lot I wanted to share — so here I am, starting again.

## Going Forward

I want this platform to be a place where I can put out the things I've been thinking about and the things I want to share.

Whenever I publish something new, I'll let you know through my Instagram stories, so I'd be glad if you'd check in from time to time.

This article doesn't have a comment section, so if you have thoughts — or something you'd like me to write about — feel free to send it to my Instagram DMs, and I'll read them.

Thanks for being here, as always. See you soon!
